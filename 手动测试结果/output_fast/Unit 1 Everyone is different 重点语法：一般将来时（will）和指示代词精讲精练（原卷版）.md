语法知识精讲

一、一般将来时（will）知识精讲

|项目|内容|
|---|---|
|概念|表示将来发生的动作、情况或打算|
|时间标志词|tomorrow, next week/year, soon, in the future, in + 一段时间|
|基本结构|肯定句：主语 + will + 动词原形 + 其他 否定句：主语 + will not (won’t) + 动词原形 + 其他 一般疑问句：Will + 主语 + 动词原形 + 其他？ 回答：Yes, 主语 + will. / No, 主语 + won’t.|
|例句|1. I will go to park tomorrow.<br>2. She won’t watch TV this evening.<br>3. Will you come to school early?<br>|


|项目|内容|
|---|---|
|用法|1. 表示将来要发生的动作<br>2. 表示临时做出的决定<br>3. 表示预测、猜想<br>|


# 二、指示代词知识精讲

|分类|单数|复数|用法|例句|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|近指|this（这个）|these（这些）|指代近处的 人或物|This is a book.These are pens.|
|远指|that（那个）|those（那些）|指代远处的 人或物|That is a tree.Those are birds.|
|注意点|1. 介绍某人用：This is…<br>2. 电话用语：This is… （我是…）；Is that…?（你 是… 吗？）<br>3. 回答时用 it/they 代替<br>|——|——|——|

#### 题型一：一般将来时 will

- 1．Michael ________ in a school in Yunnan from February to June next year. A．teaches B．taught C．will teach D．was teaching
- 2．There ________ a lecture on Henry Norman Bethune next Monday. A．will have B．will be C．is gong to have D．is having
- 3．Our school ________ a sports meeting next month. Now we’re all looking forward to it. A．holds B．will hold C．held D．is holding
- 4．—What will the weather be like tomorrow?


—The radio says there ______ a strong wind in the afternoon. A．will have B．is going to have C．will be D．is

- 5．—I think there ________ more AI teachers in our life.

—I agree. The technology (科技) is changing the way we live. A．were B．are C．will be D．will have

- 6．—Why did you put the wood near the fire? It’s dangerous.

—Don’t worry. Wet wood won’t ________. A．burnt B．be burnt C．be burning D．burn

- 7．—Will you go to Shanghai by train?

—No, _________. A．I will B．you won’t C．I won’t D．I don’t

- 8．—Where ________ you go for the summer holiday?

—I ________ Shanghai. A．do; am B．did; am C．will; am going to D．will; will

- 9．—Do you know the community library ________ free books to children next week?

—Yes! I’m going to get some storybooks for my little brother. A．gives B．gave C．will give D．has given

- 10．—Linda, do you want to watch a movie with me this evening?

—Yes. But I need to finish my homework first and then I ________ you at the cinema. A．meet B．don’t meet C．will meet D．won’t meet
